The 2 by 2 determinant

det(A) = ad − bc

Multiply down the main diagonal, then subtract the other diagonal. If the answer is zero, the matrix has no inverse.

Example: find det of [1 2; 3 4].

  1. 1·4 = 4 and 2·3 = 6.
  2. 4 − 6 = −2.
Practice: det of [2 0; 0 3]? (Not read aloud.)
